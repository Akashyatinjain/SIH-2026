A. TOLL-FREE VOICE IVR INGESTION PIPELINE (1800-180-1551)
1
Farmer Toll-Free Call: Farmer calls 1800-180-1551 from any basic feature phone (no internet required).
2
Regional Language Prompt: Telecom gateway prompts language selection (Marathi / Hindi).
3
Speech-to-Text (ASR): Audio stream converted into normalized text transcripts via speech recognition.
4
Symptom & Location Extraction: NLP model parses symptoms ("high fever", "lumps") and matches village geo-coordinates.
5
Unified Pipeline Ingestion: Creates Case #PS-2026-XXXX into the exact same PostgreSQL queue as the smartphone app.
B. OFFLINE-FIRST FIELD SYNCHRONIZATION PIPELINE (CRDT)
1
Remote Shed Data Entry: Pashu Sakhi logs cattle RFID, symptoms and photos in remote cowshed with zero cellular signal.
2
Local IndexedDB Storage: Browser-native encrypted IndexedDB stores record with SHA-256 integrity hash.
3
Signal Restoration Detection: Background ServiceWorker polls for 2G/4G or Gram Panchayat Wi-Fi connectivity.
4
Sync Manager & CRDT Resolution: Atomic batch upload reconciling timestamps to eliminate duplicate case entries.
5
Central DB Write: Transaction committed to PostgreSQL 16; hot cache updated in Redis.
C. 8-STEP DIAGNOSTIC LAB CHAIN OF CUSTODY (BSL-2/3 LIMS)
1
Clinical Order: Dr. Anand signs laboratory order in clinical portal.
2
Physical Collection: Pashu Sakhi collects nasal/scab swab using sterile collection vial.
3
Barcode Association: Barcode sticker #PS-SMP-0198 scanned & linked to RFID MH-PUN-0241.
4
Cold-Chain IoT (4.2°C): Carrier telemetry logged to TimescaleDB to ensure zero sample degradation.
5
Lab Ingress & RT-PCR Test: Aundh Lab receives sample and completes real-time Capripox PCR assay.
6
Validation & Escalation: Senior Microbiologist approves result; positive result triggers district escalation.
